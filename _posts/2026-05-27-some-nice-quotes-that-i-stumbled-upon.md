# some nice quotes that I stumbled upon

No matter what anyone says, literature has the power to affect minds.
Sometimes question them, sometimes tickle them, and in some rare
occasions change them.

Here are some that I stumbled upon during an afternoon repast or a late
night reading.

> Perhaps no one learned so painfully that to be loved and to be wanted
> are not the same thing.

- In an
  [article](https://www.bookforum.com/print/3204/blond-ambition-62801)
  about Marilyn Monroe

> Deploying a series of cricket metaphors, she said: “I’m still at the
> crease, though the bowling’s been pretty hostile of late. And in case
> anyone doubted it, can I assure you there will be no ducking the
> bouncers, no stonewalling, no playing for time? The bowling’s going to
> get hit all round the ground. That’s my style.”

- BBC article on Margaret Thatcher’s sudden resignation in 1990.
