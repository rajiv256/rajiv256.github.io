# Taming the American Blinds

If you are anything like me, you struggle with a problem for a long time
before you ask for any kind of help, and waste the youth in the process.
I am ofcourse talking about one such misstep into the fountain of youth
in my life: the classical American folding blinds. These are the ones,
where you pull on a chord and the blinds fold up into a neat little
stack at the top of the window. They are a pain to pull up, and a pain
to pull down. The cord is always tangled, and the blinds never fold up
evenly. I have spent countless hours trying to figure out how to operate
them correctly before giving up and trying to focus on more debilitating
aspects of my life. After all, I always wanted zero sunlight to enter my
room, until I realized that they are actively contributing to my
all-seasonal-depression.

So finally, I offhandedly, opened an “explainer” and it was so simple,
that it made me laugh that I wasted five years of my life in
frustration. Operating them is very simple.

**To pull the blinds up**: pull the chord down till the blinds are at
the desired height, and swing the chord to the right, hold there and
release.

**To pull the blinds down**: pull the chord down till the blinds are at
the desired height, and swing the chord to the left, hold there and
release.
